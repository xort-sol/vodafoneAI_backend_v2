# Business plans (Ireland)
Source: vodafone.ie business pages, checked 29 October 2026. Prices change: re-check before real calls.
All prices are per month. Mobile prices are before VAT; VAT-registered businesses can reclaim VAT.
Say prices in spoken form, exactly as written in quotes below. Never round, combine or invent prices.

## Business SIM Only
RED Business Unlimited SIM Only. "Twenty-five euro a month, before VAT, for the first six months, then thirty-five euro."
Twelve-month contract. Unlimited data at the fastest speed available, unlimited calls and texts to any network including the EU,
and two hundred and fifty international minutes and texts. 5G ready, free roaming across Europe.
Best for: a business that already has phones and wants to cut its mobile bill.

## Business mobile plans with a phone
Three plans, all with unlimited data and unlimited calls and texts to any network:
- RED Business Lite: "forty-five euro a month, before VAT". Includes one hundred international minutes and texts.
- RED Business Unlimited: "fifty-five euro a month, before VAT". Includes five hundred international minutes and texts.
- RED Business Unlimited Max: "eighty euro a month, before VAT". Unlimited international calls and texts, plus conferencing tools.
Phones on offer include the iPhone 17 and the Samsung S25, from free upfront, with switch-and-save offers.
Contract length and exact phone pricing: a team member will confirm.
Best for: teams that need new phones or call abroad a lot.

## Business Broadband (fibre)
Twenty-four-month contract. Current offer: save ten euro a month for the first twelve months.
- Up to 500 megabits: "forty euro a month", rising to sixty euro after the offer. Wi-Fi 5 modem. Suits a small business.
- Up to 1 gigabit, our best value: "fifty euro a month", rising to seventy euro after the offer. Wi-Fi 7 modem. Suits a medium-sized business.
- Up to 2 gigabits: "sixty-five euro a month", rising to eighty-five euro after the offer. Wi-Fi 7 modem. Suits a data-heavy business.
All include Smart Wi-Fi. Ninety-six percent of orders are installed within fourteen days; free installation depends on location.
Whether broadband prices include VAT: a team member will confirm.
Best for: offices with slow or dropping internet.

## Business Mobile Broadband (no fixed line needed)
- 4G Unlimited: "twenty-four euro thirty-nine a month, before VAT, for six months, then thirty-two fifty-two". Twelve months.
- 5G Unlimited: "thirty-two fifty-two a month, before VAT, for six months, then thirty-six fifty-nine". Twelve months.

## Good to know
- Every April, monthly prices rise by a small fixed amount (about two to three euro). If asked, say so honestly.
- Businesses with more than twenty-five staff can get custom plans: offer a team member callback.
- Anything else (custom quotes, discounts, porting, device prices, exact terms): a team member will follow up.
