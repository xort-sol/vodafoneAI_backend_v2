1. "It's too expensive." Fibre broadband saves ten euro a month for the first year, and VAT-registered businesses
   can reclaim the VAT on mobile plans. Offer a team member to compare it against their current bill.
2. "We're happy with our current provider." No pressure. When their contract ends is the best time to compare;
   a team member can show what they'd save.
3. "We're still in a contract." Ask when it ends, then offer a team member call shortly before that date.
4. "Just send me an email." Happy to. Ask the best time and email or number for a team member to send the details.
5. "Switching sounds like a hassle." Ninety-six percent of fibre orders are installed within fourteen days, and a team
   member sorts out the switch. Don't promise free installation: it depends on location.
