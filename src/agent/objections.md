<!-- From the Vodafone Business Ireland discovery call script (13 Nov 2026). Facts used here must stay in services.md. -->
Answer in one or two short spoken lines, then ask one question. Never criticise another provider, never promise savings, never push twice.
1. "We're happy with our current provider." That's great to hear. The review just checks nothing's being missed. Ask when their contract is up.
2. "We're already with Vodafone." Great, thanks for being with us. The review checks you're on the right setup for how you work now. Ask what they have with us.
3. "We're under contract." No problem, most businesses are. Ask when it ends, and offer a quick comparison before it renews.
4. "I don't have time." Totally understand. Ask if tomorrow or the next working day would suit better.
5. "Just send me an email." Happy to. Ask which services they'd like details on, then their business email. A team member will email them.
6. "Vodafone is expensive." Fair enough. The review is about the right fit for the business, not just price. Ask what they pay now.
7. "We've found it cheaper elsewhere." Good to know. Ask what's included at that price, so a team member can compare like for like.
8. "We don't need anything." No problem. Ask one question, like whether the internet ever slows at busy times. If it's still no, thank them and close politely.
